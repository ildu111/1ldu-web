About 1LDU

1LDU is the name under which Ildefons Alonso Valls presents his solo music project. His proposal is a fusion of sounds that moves between bittersweet and melancholic ambient landscapes and bursts of cosmic and joyful IDM. However, what truly defines his art is his entirely improvised "live" performance. 1LDU delves into the world of synthesizers and drum machines, creating unique soundscapes in real-time. Each performance is an unprecedented auditory journey that invites you to get lost in the music, experience sonic pleasure, and be guided by the rhythm and drive of electronic music.

Ildefons is a musician, producer, and performer with over 15 years of experience in the Catalan music scene, trained in Jazz Drumming and Composition at the Royal Academy of Music in London and Classical Piano at ESMUC in Barcelona.
