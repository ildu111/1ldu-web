Bio

1LDU is the solo project of Ildefons Alonso Valls — a fully improvised live set navigating between bittersweet ambient textures and bursts of cosmic IDM.

Each performance is built in real time: synthesizers, drum machines, and layered sequences constructed and dissolved without a fixed score. No two sets are the same.

Trained in Jazz Drumming and Composition at the Royal Academy of Music in London, and Classical Piano at ESMUC in Barcelona. Over 15 years active in the Catalan music scene.
